# PRODUCT — 가족 돌봄 운영 플랫폼

> Family Care Operations Platform. A back-office tool that lets care-center
> operators run day-to-day caregiving: care logs, settlements, insurance
> claims, and the revenue/analytics behind the service.

register: product

## Users

Primary: **돌봄 센터 운영 담당자** (care-center operators / coordinators), often
40-60 years old, not power users. They sit at a desk in a small office during
the day, or check a phone between home visits. They are responsible, detail-
oriented, and time-pressed: they need to record what care happened, calculate
what families owe, and chase insurance claims through to approval, without
fighting the software.

Secondary: **센터 관리자/원장** who looks at monthly revenue, approval rates, and
pricing to keep the service healthy.

What they need from the product:

- See today's situation the moment they open it, then act.
- Record and look things up quickly, with large, forgiving touch targets.
- Calm, plain-Korean labels. No jargon, no dashboards that feel like a trading
  terminal.
- Confidence that nothing was missed (pending claims, unpaid settlements).

## Product purpose

Replace scattered spreadsheets and memory with one place that follows the real
workflow: **방문 일정 → 돌봄 기록 → 돌봄비 정산 → 보험청구**, with **운영 분석** and
**요금제 관리** for the people running the business. Phase 1 of the redesign
separates these into distinct routed pages (away from the previous "3 pages with
nested tabs") and gives unauthenticated visitors a public commercial homepage
before asking them to log in.

## Tone

Warm, humane, and reassuring, but clean and competent. This is a care product
used by working people, not a wellness app and not clinical software. Speak like
a calm, capable colleague: short sentences, encouraging defaults, never alarmist.
Korean is the product language; English is reserved for code, identifiers, and a
single brand sub-label.

## Strategic principles

1. **Today-first.** The home screen answers "what do I need to do now?" before
   anything else.
2. **Mobile is a first-class surface**, not a shrunk desktop. Start at 320px.
3. **Calm over flashy.** Restrained presentation of numbers; no count-up theater,
   no big-number hero metrics, no gradient drama.
4. **Plain Korean, plain actions.** Every label says what happens next.
5. **Forgiving.** Empty and error states explain the situation and offer the
   next step, never a dead end.

## Anti-references (what this must NOT look like)

- Clinical healthcare SaaS: white + teal, sterile, hospital-grade density.
- Generic blue-indigo admin dashboard (the look this redesign replaces).
- Fintech "metrics terminal": dense KPI walls, gradient stat cards, dark mode.
- Wellness-app sage minimalism with nothing underneath it. The warmth must be
  earned by the neutrals and the copy, not by the accent color alone.

## Banned patterns (impeccable)

No hero-metric template (big number + label + gradient). No side-stripe colored
borders. No gradient text. No glassmorphism as default. No identical-card grids.
No em dashes in copy. No dark theme (the redesign is light-only by decision).
